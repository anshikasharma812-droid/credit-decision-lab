07 — Method

From public data to decision policy

The model is deliberately simple so the business logic remains visible. The point is decision quality—not leaderboard optimization.

STEP 01

Prepare

Pull the public UCI-derived CSV, select 10 numeric credit / repayment features, standardize using the training sample, and create a deterministic holdout set.
STEP 02

Model

Fit logistic regression with gradient descent on a bounded training sample. Produce out-of-sample PD estimates and ROC-AUC.
STEP 03

Decide

Translate probabilities into an operating threshold. Compare recall, precision, false positives, and approval rate as the cutoff moves.
